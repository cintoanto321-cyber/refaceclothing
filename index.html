Rooted in Indian Craft

Conscious Textiles, Sacred Tradition

Every thread honors generations of Indian master weavers, rainfed organic crops, and ancient natural vat dyeing.

Rainfed Indian Flax

Cultivated in harmony with monsoon rains, our organic flax requires zero heavy chemical fertilizers or depleting groundwater pumps.

Desi Botanical Dyes

Colored naturally using wild Indian indigo, turmeric, harda, and upcycled temple marigold flowers completely free from synthetic azo chemicals.

Artisan Weaver Co-Ops

Supporting rural Indian handloom clusters with fair wages, safe loom environments, and heritage preservation initiatives.

The reface Story

Celebrating Indian Craftsmanship

Founded with a vision to re-imagine traditional Indian textiles for the contemporary wardrobe, reface CLOTHING fuses relaxed modern silhouettes with ancient handloom artistry. We celebrate the organic slub, breathability, and natural texture of pure Indian linen and hand-spun khadi.
